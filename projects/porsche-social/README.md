# Porsche social-sharing media

The requested deliverable is a real Porsche clip offered through Open Graph metadata with a matching still fallback. This is publication of existing licensed footage, not a new narrated reel or manufactured shot of the shop.

Source: Kampus Production, [Pexels 8629197](https://www.pexels.com/video/a-cabriolet-car-parked-on-the-beach-8629197/), [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The local original is in `raw/` (ignored by Git). The source has no audio track. The H.264 1920×1080 clip lasts 7.2 seconds; the deployed MP4 is remuxed with fast-start metadata without re-encoding its frames.

Publication assets: `/assets/social/porsche-preview-v1.mp4` and `/assets/social/porsche-preview-v1.jpg`. The watch page is `/film/`. `content/social.json` is the source, dimensions, credit and license record. The original footage is illustrative and does not depict Houston Auto Pros, its customers or an endorsement by Porsche.
