# Project context

Houston Auto Pros is an independent premium European automotive repair brand in Houston, focused on Porsche, BMW and Mercedes-Benz owners. This is the current editable website prototype.

## Design direction

Preserve the approved graphite, gunmetal, satin steel and warm porcelain system. The influence is sculptural German sports-car design, especially the Porsche Mission design language: flowing surfaces, thin horizontal lines, precision, restrained typography and material honesty. Keep the brand independent; do not copy manufacturer marks or imply factory affiliation.

Use the files in design-reference as aesthetic references. Maintain the original H emblem with two subtly sculpted pillars and one thin crossbar. Use a flat SVG mark on the web. Metallic treatments belong mainly in physical mockups.

Homepage: cinematic dark hero, large light typography, generous warm porcelain information sections, editorial service rows, spacious approach section, and dark closing invitation. Supporting brand page documents the palette and shows CSS stationery and key-tag concepts.

## Working rules

Edit dist/index.html, dist/brand.html, dist/style.css and dist/app.js directly. No build step. Run with `python -m http.server 8080 --directory dist`. Do not introduce a framework unless requested. Keep responsive layouts, keyboard accessibility and reduced-motion behavior. Preserve the working service disclosures, request draft modal and palette copy buttons.

The request form is a browser-only draft flow, not a working booking integration. Never claim that it sends email or confirms appointments. Do not invent contact information, reviews, qualifications, warranty coverage, manufacturer affiliation or business history. Obtain verified business details for launch.

Generated hero photography is included locally. Google Fonts is an optional external typography dependency with Arial fallback. No secrets or hosting IDs are required.
